v7 update: Six XGBoost assumption fixes were applied. Most importantly: group_size_tier is now encoded as is_meeting_only (binary) + group_size_tier_clean_enc (4 ordered tiers); market_segment is now 13 one-hot columns. 194 meeting-only records (room_block=0) are excluded from ADR training. F&B R² improved to 0.969.

Original finding: Of 44 APIT document variables mapped to dataset columns, only 8 survive into the final base feature pool untouched. The majority are removed by correlation pruning — they are highly redundant with engineered time-series features that carry the same information in a denser form (e.g. total_room_nights_requested is r=0.92 with proposed_total_revenue). Fourteen additional variables are force-added back into the pickup and F&B models because the ADR-proxy screening incorrectly ranked them low.

Proxy bias: The importance screening in step 4 uses quoted_adr as a proxy target to rank features. This under-ranks features that predict pickup or F&B but are irrelevant to pricing — such as event_type_enc, which explains 34% of pickup variance but ranked 127th out of 181 by the ADR proxy. These are force-added back per model (see §Force-Added Variables).

Flag: rate below baseline Flag_Rate_Below_Baseline APIT Derived ✓ In Model ADRConvF&BPickup The single most important feature in the ADR model (42% importance). Flags when client's stated rate is >3% below the property's historical ADR for those dates. A nonlinear threshold signal not captured by continuous rate features.
Meeting space ratio Meeting_Space_Ratio APIT Derived ✓ In Model ADRConvF&BPickup Meeting rooms per 10 attendees. Survives all pruning (VIF=3.42). Captures the intensity of space use relative to group size — groups needing lots of rooms per person tend to have higher F&B spend and different rate sensitivity.
ADR discount percentage ADR_Discount_Pct APIT Derived Dropped: Correlation r = 0.932 (|r|) with standard_rate. ADR discount = (baseline − quoted) / baseline. Since baseline varies little within market segments, the discount percentage is nearly a linear function of the quoted rate, which is collinear with standard_rate. Flag_Rate_Below_Baseline captures the nonlinear threshold aspect that survives.
Revenue per room night Revenue_Per_Room_Night APIT Derived Dropped: VIF Removed at VIF stage. Revenue_Per_Room_Night = proposed_total_revenue / total_room_nights ≈ quoted_adr, making it nearly collinear with the ADR target itself and with proposed_total_revenue. The correlation is only r=0.08 with revenue but VIF within the full feature matrix is high.

Why does this work? Force-adding variables that were removed by correlation pruning does NOT violate the pruning logic — correlation pruning only ran on the base candidate pool. The force-adds are appended after pruning, for specific models only. If two force-added variables are themselves correlated with each other, the model's regularisation (L1/L2 penalties tuned by Optuna) handles the remaining redundancy without causing coefficient instability, because XGBoost's tree structure is inherently more robust to multicollinearity than linear models.
