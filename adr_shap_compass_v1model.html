Method: shap.TreeExplainer on the uploaded adr_model_rebuilt.pkl, run against rfp_training_data_complete_v3_with_transient.csv filtered to room_block > 0 (n=1705). Target is quoted_adr. KNOWN ISSUE in this specific model: two of its 51 features are mislabeled proxies, not the real pipeline's definitions — tr_demand_tier here is actually the existing Demand_Tier column's category code (the real tr_demand_tier is a distinct 5-level field from a separate transient-demand file), and tr_rooms_to_capacity here is room_block / Rooms_Available_Peak_Date (the real one is that same file's own Rooms_to_Capacity column). Both are included in axis/contributor selection as-is, since that's what this model actually learned from — tr_demand_tier in fact lands as one of the top-3 axes below. Four further features (tr_transient_adr, tr_transient_revpar, tr_pace_vs_prior_year, tr_transient_pace_30d) were trained as always-missing and are excluded from axis selection and the contributor panel, since SHAP on a constant missing value isn't a real read on their effect. Axes are the top 3 by mean |SHAP| among the remaining 47, scaled by each axis's own max |SHAP|. Hover-panel bars show each point's real top-5 signed SHAP contributions in ADR dollars.
