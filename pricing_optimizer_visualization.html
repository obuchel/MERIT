Is This Better Than XGBoost?

This is not a replacement for XGBoost. It is a prescriptive layer on top of XGBoost. The ADR XGBoost model predicts price/value; the conversion XGBoost model predicts win probability; the optimizer asks what price and allocation maximize profit under capacity.
